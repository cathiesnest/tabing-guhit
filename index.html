Before you apply

Protect yourself. Know your value.

🚩 Never pay to get a job.

A legitimate remote job should not require a joining fee, recruitment fee, application fee, or payment simply to be hired.

Verify the employer independently.

Know your value.

Don't automatically accept a low-ball offer just because you need a sideline. Research reasonable market rates and negotiate professionally.

Starting small is okay. Undervaluing yourself is not.
